# 📊 Sales Dashboard — Microsoft Excel

An interactive sales analysis dashboard created using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer activity, monthly trends, and geographic performance.

> **Project Type:** Guided Data Analytics Project
> **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
> **Dataset:** Sales transaction data
> **Records:** 9,994
> **Period:** 2014–2017

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to transform raw sales transaction data into an interactive Excel dashboard that could be used to explore sales and profit performance from multiple business perspectives.

The project focuses on developing practical skills in:

* Data preparation
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Data aggregation
* Trend analysis
* Geographic analysis
* Customer analysis
* Dashboard design
* Business insight generation

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales by Category

Sales were analyzed across the three major categories:

* Furniture
* Office Supplies
* Technology

Technology generated the highest sales at approximately **$836K**.

---

### 2. Sales by Sub-Category

The analysis was further drilled down into individual product sub-categories.

Some of the highest-selling sub-categories included:

* Phones
* Chairs
* Storage
* Tables
* Binders

This demonstrates how category-level analysis can be extended into more detailed product-level analysis.

---

### 3. Profit Performance Over Time

Profit was analyzed across the four-year period and compared across the major categories.

Technology consistently generated strong profit performance, while Furniture showed a significant difference between its sales volume and profitability.

---

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis

Monthly sales were analyzed to identify periods of stronger and weaker sales activity.

The analysis showed particularly strong sales activity toward the later months of the year, with November and December among the highest-sales months in the dataset.

---

### 5. Geographic Analysis

Sales were analyzed across U.S. states.

California generated the highest sales at approximately **$458K**.

This provides a starting point for investigating geographic sales concentration and regional performance.

---

### 6. Customer Analysis

The dashboard also examines customer activity and identifies the top customers based on profit contribution.

## 🔎 Key Insights

### Revenue does not equal profitability

Furniture generated approximately **$742K in sales**, but its profit was only around **$18.5K**.

This highlights an important analytical principle:

> High revenue does not necessarily mean high profitability.

---

### Technology was a major contributor

Technology generated approximately:

**$836K Sales**
**$145K Profit**

This made it the strongest-performing major category in the dataset by both sales and profit.

---

### Sales were geographically concentrated

California was the highest-sales state with approximately **$458K** in sales.

This raises potential business questions around regional demand, customer concentration, and market opportunities.

---

### Seasonality is visible

Monthly analysis shows stronger sales activity toward the end of the year, particularly during November and December.

This could be useful when thinking about inventory planning, promotions, staffing, and sales forecasting.
